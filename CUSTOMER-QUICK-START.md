# Quick Start Guide for Customers

## 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Choose Your Computer
Pick any computer in your office that:
- Is on the same network as your camera
- Can stay on during business hours

### Step 2: Install Bridge (10 minutes)
We'll help you install a small program that runs in the background. It requires no daily attention.

### Step 3: Configure Camera (5 minutes)
In your camera settings, point it to your office computer instead of the internet.

**That's it!** Your camera will now automatically send license plate data to our web application.
